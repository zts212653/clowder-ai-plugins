## Data rollback and remaining boundary

The packaged [deployment instructions](../packages/collective-connector/DEPLOYMENT.md#alpha25-邀请数据与回退)
specify the stop/whole-directory backup/copy/convert/validate/restart sequence.
alpha.24 and alpha.22 reject new invitation fields. The existing alpha.25 helper
supports only those exact old targets, saves creator/revocation metadata privately,
and makes revoked invites consumed so the old binary still refuses them. Re-upgrade
restores metadata onto the **latest old-version state**, preserving its new writes
and consumed markers. Downgrading a catalog entry or executable alone cannot do this.

The helper's source regression passed here; PR182 separately records the original
published alpha.22/alpha.24 reader probes. The reviewer's verbatim probe and both
old archive inputs were copied out of the session temp directory into r2
`rollback-originals/`, with SHA256 and provenance in `preservation.json`. This
preservation is not claimed as a fresh 26-case probe run or a relocatable runner. Production stop/backup/conversion and
real-provider/friend acceptance have not been executed by this preparation.
Actual production deployment remains with its existing owner and authorization.
